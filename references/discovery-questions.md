# Discovery questions

Use this as a menu, not a script. Ask only what the existing material doesn't already answer, max 6 questions per batch, each with a suggested default so the user can reply "yes to all" or correct only what's wrong.

## Question bank by purpose

**The business**

- What do you sell or do, in one sentence a customer would use?
- What is the single most profitable or most wanted offer to lead with?

**The audience**

- Who is the ideal customer, and what are they trying to fix when they first look for you?
- Where do they come from (Google, Instagram, ads, word of mouth)? This decides how much trust the page must build from scratch.

**The promise and difference**

- What does a customer have after working with you that they didn't have before?
- Why would someone pick you over the two closest alternatives? (Push for specific and true: speed, method, results, guarantee, location, experience.)

**Proof**

- What real proof exists today: testimonials, results, case studies, client logos, years in business, numbers you can stand behind?
- If none yet: what could be collected quickly (2-3 client quotes, before/after, a portfolio piece)?

**The action**

- What is the one thing a visitor should do? (Book a call, request a quote, buy, register.) What happens right after they do it?

**Tone and limits**

- Three adjectives for how the brand should sound. One example of a brand that sounds right, and one that sounds wrong.
- Anything that must or must not be said: prices shown or hidden, legal wording, banned words, regulated claims.

**Format**

- Which pages, in which languages (French, English, both)? Is there existing content to reuse or replace?

## Defaults to propose when the user is unsure

- Tone: clear, calm, professional, no hype.
- Primary action: book a free call / request a quote.
- Proof: placeholders for testimonials and results until the client supplies them.
- Pricing: "on request" with a placeholder range, unless the client prefers to show prices.

## Reflecting back

After the answers, restate the vision in the Vision Brief format and ask a single confirmation question: "Is this the right direction before I research and write?" Fix anything wrong before moving on.

## Using AskUserQuestion

Each question needs a short header and 2-4 options that are realistic answers for this kind of business, with the likeliest first. The user can always type their own answer, so options are prompts, not limits. Example for "core promise": "More inquiries from people who find us online", "A professional image that builds trust", "A page that converts our ad traffic", with free text for anything else. Never ask more than 4 questions in one round, and never ask something the user already answered.
